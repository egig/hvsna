## Welcome to Hvsna Documentation

Hvsna is a **prayer-first task app** that helps Muslims organize their daily tasks around the five daily prayers. Unlike traditional productivity apps, Hvsna puts prayer times at the center of your routine while displaying Hijri dates alongside the civil calendar.
