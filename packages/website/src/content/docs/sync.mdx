## Why Sync?

Syncing your Hvsna data across devices ensures:

- **Consistency**: Same tasks on phone, tablet, and web
- **Backup**: Data is safely stored in the cloud
- **Recovery**: Restore data if you lose your device
- **Multi-device workflow**: Plan on tablet, execute on phone

## Setting Up Sync
1. Hvsna Sync is enabled by default when you create an account. However the features is currently invite-only.
   If you would like to try it out, please contact us at support@hvsna.com


### Sign In on Other Devices

1. Download Hvsna on your other device
2. Open **Settings → Account → Sign In**
3. Enter the same email and password
4. Your data will automatically sync

## What Gets Synced?

### Synchronized Data

All your data syncs across devices:

- ✅ Tasks (all states: active, completed)
- ✅ Tags
- ✅ App Settings(language)

## How Sync Works

### Automatic Sync

Changes sync automatically when:

- You create, complete, or edit a task
- You modify settings
- You open the app (catches up on missed changes)
- App is backgrounded and reopened

### Manual Sync

Force a sync if needed:

1. Go to *Settings* → *Sync*
2. Tap "Sync Now" to do the sync manually.

## Sync Troubleshooting

**Sync not working?**

1. Check internet connection on all devices
2. Verify you're signed into the same account
3. Try manual sync (pull down to refresh)
4. Sign out and back in
5. Contact support if issues persist

**Data looks different on devices?**

- Wait a moment for sync to complete
- Pull down to force refresh
- Check if one device is offline
- Review conflict resolution settings

**Want to stop syncing?**

1. Go to *Settings* → *Account* → *Sign Out*
2. Your local data remains
3. Cloud copy is retained (can delete in account settings)
