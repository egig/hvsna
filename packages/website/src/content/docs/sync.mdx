## Why Sync?

Syncing your Hvsna data across devices ensures:

- **Consistency**: Same tasks on Android and Web
- **Backup**: Data is safely stored in the cloud
- **Recovery**: Restore data if you lose your device
- **Multi-device workflow**: Plan on Web, execute on Android (or the other way around)

## Setting Up Sync

Sync is a paid feature, available on both Android and Web.

1. Create an account (or sign in) and verify your email — sync stays off until your email is confirmed
2. Go to **Settings → Subscription** and tap **Upgrade to Sync**
3. Complete checkout — you're redirected back to the app once it's done
4. Sync turns on automatically

### Sign In on Other Devices

1. Open Hvsna on your other device (Android app or [app.hvsna.com](https://app.hvsna.com))
2. Sign in with the same email and password
3. Your data syncs automatically

## What Gets Synced?

### Synchronized Data

- ✅ Tasks (all states: active, completed)
- ✅ Recurring tasks
- ✅ Tags
- ✅ App settings (language)

## How Sync Works

### Automatic Sync

Changes sync automatically when:

- You create, complete, or edit a task
- You modify settings
- You open the app (catches up on missed changes)

### Manual Sync

Force a sync if needed:

1. Go to **Settings → Sync**
2. Tap **Sync Now**

## Sync Troubleshooting

**Sync not working?**

1. Check internet connection on all devices
2. Verify your email is confirmed (check for a verification banner in Settings → Sync)
3. Verify you're signed into the same account on both devices
4. Try a manual sync (**Settings → Sync → Sync Now**)
5. Contact support if issues persist

**Data looks different on devices?**

- Wait a moment for sync to complete
- Trigger a manual sync
- Check if one device is offline

**Want to stop syncing?**

1. Go to **Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription** to cancel
2. Your local data on each device remains
3. Cloud copy is retained (can be deleted from account settings)
