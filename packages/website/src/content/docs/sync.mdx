## What Sync Does

Sync keeps the same tasks, recurring tasks, tags, and app settings on every
device you sign in on — Android and Web. Your data is also stored on our server,
so you can recover it on a new device.

Sync is a **paid feature** and requires a **verified email address**.

## Turning Sync On

1. Create an account and verify your email — sync stays off until it's confirmed.
   You can resend the verification email from **Settings → Sync**.
2. Subscribe to the **Sync** plan:

   <PlatformTabs>
   <Web>

   Go to **Settings → Subscription**, tap **Upgrade to Sync**, and complete
   checkout (hosted by Lemon Squeezy). You're returned to the app and sync
   turns on automatically.

   </Web>
   <Android>

   Subscribe from the web app at [app.hvsna.com](https://app.hvsna.com) using
   the same account (**Settings → Subscription → Upgrade to Sync**).

   </Android>
   </PlatformTabs>

3. Sign in with the same email and password on your other devices — your data
   downloads automatically.

## What Gets Synced

- Tasks (active and completed)
- Recurring task templates
- Tags
- App settings (language, location, prayer calculation method and madhab, Hijri
  date offsets, notification preferences; timezone on web)

Prayer times themselves aren't synced — each device computes them locally from
the synced location and settings.

## How It Works

Changes sync automatically as you make them. You can also force a sync and see
the last sync time and status from the Sync screen.

<PlatformTabs>
<Web>

Also syncs when you open the app and when you reconnect. Force one from
**Settings → Sync → Sync Now**.

</Web>
<Android>

Also syncs when you sign in, when you reconnect, when you pull to refresh, and
every 15 minutes in the background. Force one from **Settings → Sync** (pull to
refresh or **Sync now**).

</Android>
</PlatformTabs>

If the same item was changed on two devices, the most recent change wins. There's
no conflict prompt.

## Troubleshooting

**Sync not working**

1. Check each device is online
2. Confirm your email is verified (look for the prompt on the Sync screen)
3. Confirm you're signed into the same account everywhere
4. Run a manual sync and read the status message

**Data looks different between devices**

- Run a manual sync on both and give it a moment
- Check neither device is offline

**Stopping sync**

1. Cancel the plan from **Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription** (web) —
   this opens your Lemon Squeezy portal
2. Local data stays on each device; sync stops at the end of the billing period
3. To remove the server copy, contact **support@hvsna.com**
