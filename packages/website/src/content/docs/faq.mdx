## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Is Hvsna free?**

A: Yes, Hvsna has a free version with unlimited tasks   

**Q: Which platforms is Hvsna available on?**

A: Android and Web (at [app.hvsna.com](https://app.hvsna.com)). An iOS version is coming soon.

**Q: Do I need an account to use Hvsna?**

A: No, you can use Hvsna without an account on Android or Web. An account, with an email-verified Sync subscription, is only needed for syncing across devices.

**Q: Is my data private?**

A: Yes. Your task data is stored locally on your device. With an account, it's encrypted in the cloud. We never sell or share your data.

### Prayer Times

**Q: Why are my prayer times different from my local masjid?**

A: Small variations (1-5 minutes) are normal due to:
- Different calculation methods
- Slight differences in coordinates
- Masjid may use local sighting vs. calculation


**Q: Prayer times seem completely wrong?**

A: Check these in order:
1. **Location**: Verify correct in Settings
2. **Time Zone**: Ensure your phone's timezone is correct

If still wrong, contact support with your location and expected times.

**Q: Can I set custom prayer times?**

A: Not directly. Hvsna calculates times based on your location and chosen method. For masjid-specific times, note them manually in tasks.

### Tasks & Reminders

**Q: Can I set tasks without linking to prayer times?**

A: Yes! When creating a task, simply don't select a prayer association. You can set specific clock times instead.

**Q: How do recurring tasks work?**

A: Set a task to repeat daily, weekly, monthly, or custom. See **Recurring Tasks** in docs for details.

**Q: Why am I not getting notifications?**

A: Check these:
1. **App permissions**: Hvsna needs notification permission in system settings
2. **Do Not Disturb**: May be silencing alerts
3. **Battery optimization**: Some phones restrict background apps
4. **In-app settings**: Verify notifications enabled in **Settings → Notifications**

See **Notification Settings** guide for detailed troubleshooting.


### Sync & Account

**Q: How do I sync between my phone and tablet?**

A: Sign in to the same account on both devices. Data syncs automatically. See **Syncing Across Devices** guide.

**Q: I lost my phone. Can I recover my data?**

A: If you had an account and sync enabled, simply sign in on your new device. Without an account, data was stored only on the lost phone.


**Q: Can I use Hvsna on multiple devices without syncing?**

A: Yes, but they'll have separate data. Install and use independently without signing in.


## Troubleshooting

### App Won't Open

**Problem**: Hvsna crashes or won't start.

**Solutions**:
1. Force close and reopen
2. Restart your phone
3. Update to latest app version
4. Reinstall app (data will restore if you have an account)
5. Contact support if persists

### Incorrect Location

**Problem**: App shows wrong city or prayer times are off.

**Solutions**:
1. **Settings → Location → Detect Automatically**
2. If wrong, tap location and search for correct city
3. Verify coordinates if using manual entry
4. Check that timezone matches your location

### Notifications Not Working

**Problem**: Not getting prayer or task alerts.

**Checklist**:
- [ ] Notifications enabled in Hvsna (**Settings → Notifications**)
- [ ] System notification permission granted
- [ ] Do Not Disturb is off (or Hvsna is whitelisted)
- [ ] Battery optimization disabled for Hvsna
- [ ] Phone volume is up
- [ ] Correct notification sound selected

**iOS-specific**: Check **Settings → Notifications → Hvsna**
**Android-specific**: Check **Settings → Apps → Hvsna → Notifications**

### Sync Issues

**Problem**: Changes not appearing on other devices.

**Solutions**:
1. Ensure both devices are online
2. Pull down to refresh (force sync)
3. Verify signed into same account
4. Check **Settings → Sync** for errors
5. Sign out and back in on problematic device
6. Contact support if data appears lost

### Prayer Time Calculation Errors

**Problem**: Times are impossible (e.g., Fajr after sunrise).

**Solutions**:
1. Check location is correct (not default/test location)
2. Verify calculation method is appropriate for your region
3. Check for extreme latitudes (near poles have special cases)
4. Report to support with location and screenshot

### Battery Drain

**Problem**: Hvsna using too much battery.

**Solutions**:
1. Disable background app refresh (iOS) or background activity (Android)
2. Reduce notification frequency
3. Ensure location is set manually (GPS uses more battery)
4. Check for app updates that may optimize performance

## Getting Help

### Contact Support

Still having issues? Contact us:

- **Email**: support@hvsna.com
- **Response time**: Usually within 24 hours
- **Include**: Your device type, app version, and detailed description

### Report a Bug

Help us improve:

1. **Settings → Help & Feedback → Report Bug**
2. Describe what happened
3. Include steps to reproduce
4. Attach screenshots if helpful

### Feature Requests

Have an idea?

- Email support@hvsna.com with subject "Feature Request"
- Describe the feature and why it would help
- We review all suggestions
