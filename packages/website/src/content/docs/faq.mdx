## General

**Is Hvsna free?**

Yes — the free version has unlimited tasks, prayer-anchored scheduling, Hijri
awareness, and full offline use.

**Which platforms?**

Android today. iOS is coming soon.

**Do I need an account?**

No. Hvsna has no accounts.

**Is my data private?**

Your tasks are stored only on your device. We don't sell or share your data.

## Prayer Times

**Why are my times a few minutes off from my masjid?**

Different calculation methods, slightly different coordinates, or a masjid using
local sighting. Set the [calculation method](/help/general-settings) your local
authority uses.

**My prayer times look completely wrong.**

1. Check your location in **Settings → General → Location**
2. Check your device timezone
3. Check the calculation method suits your region

**Can I set custom prayer times?**

Not directly — Hvsna calculates them from your location, method, and madhab. For
masjid-specific times, add them as timed tasks.

## Tasks & Reminders

**Can I have tasks that aren't tied to a prayer?**

Yes — give a task a clock time, or no time at all (it waits in your Inbox).

**How do recurring tasks work?**

Daily / weekly / monthly / yearly, with an optional interval and end condition,
on the civil calendar. See [Recurring Tasks](/help/recurring-tasks).

**Why am I not getting notifications?**

Only tasks with a specific clock time get a reminder. Then check the system
notification permission, Do Not Disturb, and (Android) battery optimization. See [Notifications](/help/notifications).

## Data

**I lost my phone — can I get my data back?**

Only if you exported a backup (**Settings → Backup & Restore → Export data**).
Otherwise the data was only on that phone.

## Troubleshooting

**The app won't open or is misbehaving**

1. Close and reopen it
2. Update to the latest version
3. As a last resort, **Settings → Reset Device Data** (erases local data)
4. Contact support if it persists

**Wrong city / wrong prayer times**

**Settings → General → Location** → search for the correct place, or
**Use current location**. Check your timezone matches.

## Getting Help

Email **support@hvsna.com** with your platform, app version (**Settings →
About**), and what happened. Feature ideas are welcome at the same address.
