## Why Location Matters

Accurate prayer times depend on your precise geographical position. Hvsna uses your location to calculate:

- Exact prayer times for your coordinates
- Local sunrise and sunset times
- Seasonal variations in prayer schedules

## Setting Your Location

- There is no settings page for location, it is automatically detected when you first open the app.
If you do not  grant location permission, you will be prompted to manually set your location.


## Updating Your Location

- Tap the locatoin name, it will re-run auto detection for your current location, otherwise it will allow you to manually set your location.


## Location Privacy

Your privacy is important:

- Location data is stored **only on your device**
- We do not track or store your location on our servers
- Coordinates are used solely for prayer time calculations
- You can clear location data anytime in Settings
