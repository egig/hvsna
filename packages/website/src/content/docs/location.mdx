## Why Location Matters

Prayer times depend on the sun's position at your coordinates, so Hvsna needs to
know where you are. It's also used to pick your timezone.

## First Launch

When you first open Hvsna it asks for location permission:

- **Allow** — it detects your position and reverse-geocodes it to a place name
- **Don't allow** — it opens a search box so you can pick your city manually

If detection fails for any reason, Hvsna falls back to the Jakarta area until you
set a location.

## Changing Your Location

<PlatformTabs>
<Web>

Open **Settings → General → Location**.

</Web>
<Android>

Open **Settings → Location**.

</Android>
</PlatformTabs>

- Tap the location name to **search** for a different place
- Tap **Use current location** to re-run GPS detection

Once you've set a location manually, Hvsna won't re-prompt for GPS on its own.

## Timezone

Your timezone is set from your location automatically. On the web app you can
override it in **Settings → General → Timezone**; on Android it follows your
device.

## Privacy

- Your location is stored on your device (and, if you sync, in your account)
- It's sent to our geocoding proxy only to turn coordinates into a place name
- It's used only for prayer time and timezone calculation
- **Settings → Reset Device Data** clears it along with everything else
