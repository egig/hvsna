## How Times Are Calculated

Hvsna computes prayer times **on your device** from:

- Your **location** (latitude and longitude)
- The **calculation method** and **madhab** in [Settings](/help/general-settings)

Times are never fetched from a server, so they work offline.

## The Five Daily Prayers

| Prayer | Time |
|--------|------|
| **Fajr** | Dawn — ends at sunrise |
| **Dhuhr** | Just after the sun passes its zenith |
| **Asr** | Afternoon — depends on your madhab |
| **Maghrib** | Sunset |
| **Isha** | Nightfall |

Sunrise is also shown, as a marker between Fajr and Dhuhr.

## Calculation Method

The method sets the sun-below-horizon angles used for Fajr and Isha. Different
regions and organizations use different conventions, which is the main reason two
apps can disagree by a few minutes. Pick the one your local authority uses.

**KEMENAG** is the Indonesian Ministry of Religious Affairs method. Hvsna builds
it from 20° / 18° angles plus the Kemenag *ihtiyati* safety margin (+2 minutes on
each prayer, −2 minutes on sunrise) so it matches the printed Bimas Islam
schedules.

## Madhab

The madhab (Shafi or Hanafi) only affects **Asr**: Hanafi begins Asr later, when
an object's shadow is twice its length plus the noon shadow.

## Why Don't These Match My Masjid Exactly?

Small differences (1–5 minutes) are normal — different calculation methods,
slightly different coordinates, or a masjid that goes by local sighting rather
than calculation. If times are wildly off, check your location and your device
timezone.
