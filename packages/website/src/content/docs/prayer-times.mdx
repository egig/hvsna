## How Location Affects Prayer Times

Prayer times are determined by the position of the sun relative to your location on Earth. Hvsna calculates precise times based on:

- **Latitude & Longitude**: Your exact position on the globe


## The Five Daily Prayers

Hvsna displays the times for each of the five obligatory prayers:

| Prayer | Time | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **Fajr** | Dawn | Begins at true dawn (twilight), ends at sunrise |
| **Dhuhr** | Noon | Begins when sun passes zenith, ends when shadow equals object height |
| **Asr** | Afternoon | Begins when shadow equals object height + noon shadow |
| **Maghrib** | Sunset | Begins at sunset, ends at twilight disappearance |
| **Isha** | Night | Begins at twilight disappearance, ends at true dawn |
