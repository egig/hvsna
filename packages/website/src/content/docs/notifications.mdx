## What Gets a Reminder

Hvsna can remind you before a task that has a specific **clock time**. Tasks with
no time, and prayer-anchored tasks, don't get a reminder — there's no fixed
instant to schedule against. There are no prayer-time notifications.

## Turning Reminders On

<PlatformTabs>
<Web>

1. Open **Settings → Notifications**
2. Turn on **Enable notifications** — the browser asks for permission the first time
3. Pick how far ahead to be reminded (5, 10, 15, 30, or 60 minutes) — this one
   lead time applies to every timed task
4. Use **Send test notification** to check it works

Web reminders are scheduled inside the browser, so **the app tab has to stay
open** for them to fire, and they're only set for tasks more than an hour away.

</Web>
<Android>

1. Open **Settings → Reminders**
2. Turn on the master switch — Android asks for notification permission and,
   on newer versions, permission to schedule exact alarms
3. On each task with a clock time, open it and turn on **Reminder**, then choose
   when: at the time, or 5 minutes / 15 minutes / 1 hour / 1 day before

Android reminders fire in the background and survive a reboot. The notification
has a **Mark done** action.

</Android>
</PlatformTabs>

If you previously denied permission, re-enable it in your system settings for
Hvsna.

## Not Getting Notifications?

<PlatformTabs>
<Web>

1. Check the site's notification permission in your browser
2. Check Do Not Disturb / focus modes
3. Confirm notifications are on in **Settings → Notifications**
4. Confirm the Hvsna tab is still open

</Web>
<Android>

1. Check the notification permission for Hvsna in system settings
2. Check that "Alarms & reminders" / exact-alarm permission is allowed
3. Check Do Not Disturb and battery optimization aren't restricting Hvsna
4. Confirm the master switch is on in **Settings → Reminders**, and the task's
   own **Reminder** switch is on

</Android>
</PlatformTabs>
