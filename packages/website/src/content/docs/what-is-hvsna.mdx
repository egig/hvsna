## What is Hvsna?

Hvsna is a **prayer-first task app** for Muslims who want to plan their day
around the five daily prayers while staying aware of the Islamic calendar.

## Key Ideas

- **Prayer-anchored scheduling** — give a task a prayer instead of a clock time
  (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha), and it sorts into that slot for the day
- **Hijri awareness** — the Hijri date is shown alongside the civil date, with
  per-month adjustments so it matches your local moon sighting
- **Local first** — tasks and prayer times live on your device and work offline
- **Optional sync** — a paid Sync plan keeps everything in step across Android
  and Web

## Who It's For

- Anyone structuring their day around salah
- People building habits tied to prayer times
- Students and professionals who want a simple, faith-aware planner

## Prayer Times

Prayer times are computed on the device from your location and your chosen
calculation method and madhab — never fetched. See
[Prayer Times](/help/prayer-times).

## Platforms

Android and Web today.
