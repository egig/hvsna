## What is Hvsna?

Hvsna is a **prayer-first task app** designed specifically for Muslims who want to organize their daily tasks around prayer times. Unlike traditional todo apps, Hvsna puts prayer times at the center of your productivity while keeping you aware of the Islamic calendar.

## Key Features

- **Prayer-Based Scheduling**: Create tasks tied to specific prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha)
- **Hijri Calendar Awareness**: View Hijri dates displayed alongside the civil calendar
- **Cross-Device Sync**: Access your tasks on Android and Web with a Sync plan

## Who is Hvsna For?

Hvsna is perfect for:

- Muslims looking to structure their day around the five daily prayers
- Anyone wanting to build consistent habits tied to prayer times
- Busy professionals who need reminders that align with their faith
- Students managing their study schedule around prayer times
- Families coordinating household tasks and prayer schedules

## Prayer-First Design

Most productivity apps treat prayer as an interruption. Hvsna is different:

1. **Prayer times anchor your schedule** — Plan tasks between Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha
2. **Faith-centered productivity** — Your deen comes first, productivity follows naturally
3. **Hijri awareness built-in** — Islamic dates displayed alongside civil calendar to keep you connected to Islamic time

## Hijri Calendar Awareness

While you schedule using the civil calendar (Gregorian), Hvsna keeps you aware of the Islamic calendar by:

- Displaying the **Hijri date** alongside the civil calendar date
- Showing **Islamic months** like Ramadan, Dhul-Hijjah, etc.
- Helping you track habits and tasks across **both calendar systems**

This dual-calendar approach helps you stay productive in daily life while remaining conscious of the Islamic calendar.
