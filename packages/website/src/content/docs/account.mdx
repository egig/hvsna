## Do I Need an Account?

No. Hvsna works fully without an account — your tasks, settings, and prayer
times are stored on your device.

Accounts exist only in the **web app**, for one reason: **syncing your data
across browsers**, which also requires a paid Sync plan and a verified email
address. See [Syncing Across Devices](/help/sync). The **Android app has no
accounts or sync** — it is fully offline.

## Creating an Account

1. Open **Settings → Sign In** (web app)
2. Choose **Sign Up**
3. Enter your email and a password
4. Check your inbox for a verification link and open it

New accounts are **unverified** until you open that link. A banner in the app
reminds you, and you can resend the email from **Settings → Sync**.

## Signing In on Another Device

1. Open [app.hvsna.com](https://app.hvsna.com) in the other browser
2. Open **Settings → Sign In**
3. Enter the same email and password

If you have an active Sync plan, your data downloads automatically.

## Your Profile

**Settings → Account** shows your email, account ID, and the date you joined.
This screen is currently read-only.

## Signing Out

**Settings → Sign Out**. Your local data stays on the device; signing back in
reconnects it to sync.

## Subscription

The **Sync** plan is managed from the web app. **Settings → Subscription**
shows your current plan (Free or Sync), status, and renewal or end date.

- **Upgrade** — tap **Upgrade to Sync**. Checkout is hosted by Lemon Squeezy;
  you're returned to the app and Sync turns on automatically.
- **Cancel or update payment** — tap **Manage Subscription** or **Update Payment
  Method** to open your Lemon Squeezy customer portal. If you cancel, Sync keeps
  working until the end of the current billing period.

## Password Reset & Account Deletion

These aren't available in the app yet. Email **support@hvsna.com** and we'll
help:

- **Forgot your password** — we'll send you a reset link
- **Delete your account** — we'll remove your account and its synced copy

To clear data from a single device without deleting your account:

<PlatformTabs>
<Web>

Use **Settings → Reset Device Data** (see [Offline Use](/help/offline)).

</Web>
<Android>

Use **Settings → Backup & Restore** to export a copy first if you want one, then
clear the app's storage in Android system settings.

</Android>
</PlatformTabs>

## Account Recovery

If you can't access your email, contact **support@hvsna.com** with what you can
recall about the account (approximate sign-up date, devices used, recent task
content) so we can verify ownership.
